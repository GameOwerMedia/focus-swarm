# Recon brief: Larder

Target: the Larder demo site, served locally (`examples/demo-site`). Four pages:
home, pricing, sign-up, privacy. Recon by the orchestrator before casting.

**What it is.** A shared household pantry app. One live list of what is in the
fridge and cupboards, shared by everyone in a home (flatshares, couples,
families). Items are added by scanning barcodes or supermarket receipts; the app
sends expiry nudges with recipes, builds a shopping list from what runs low, and
on the top plan produces a monthly waste report.

**What it claims.** "Know what's in the fridge before you shop." Most
households "save more than the subscription". Receipt scanning works with
"most major supermarket receipts in the US and UK".

**What the site actually shows.** A marketing home page with a phone mock-up,
six feature tiles, one anonymous beta quote, and a "See how it works" button
that scrolls to a placeholder ("Product tour video coming soon"). No app store
badges: iOS is "soon", Android "later this year", the web app "works today",
but no link opens the web app. The nav button "Get the app" goes to the
sign-up form.

**Pricing and access.** Starter free (1 member, 50 items). Household $3.99/mo
and Family+ $6.99/mo, both marked with an asterisk: billed annually ($47.88 and
$83.88). Monthly prices ($5.99, $9.99) only appear inside a collapsed FAQ. The
14-day trial requires a card and converts to the annual plan unless cancelled.

**Sign-up.** One page, three steps shown (account, payment, invite). Email,
password and full card details on the first screen. Consent to "partner
offers" is bundled into the terms line under the button.

**Privacy.** Collects pantry items, receipts (store, date, items, prices),
household members' names and emails, analytics. Receipt images kept 30 days.
"May share anonymised and aggregated shopping insights with retail and brand
partners." Deletion by email, within 30 days. Everyone in a household sees who
added each item.

**Stage and model.** Pre-launch or early beta, two founders implied by the
pitch context, consumer subscription plus a data or retail-media revenue line
(partner offers).

**Domain vocabulary.** Pantry, expiry nudge, receipt OCR, household seats,
shopping list sync, food waste, retail media, loyalty data.

**Broken or thin.** Video placeholder; no route into the web app from the site;
no screenshots of the real product; the testimonial is unattributed; the
"saves more than the subscription" claim has no number behind it.
