Questions

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Monthly billing is available at $5.99 (Household) and $9.99 (Family+) per month. Switch in the app under Settings, Billing.

Why do you need my card for the free trial?

It keeps the trial smooth for your whole household. We will email you three days before your trial ends.

Does it work offline?

You can view your pantry offline. Changes sync when you are back online.

Which supermarkets do you support?

Receipt scanning works with most major supermarket receipts in the US and UK. More countries are coming.
