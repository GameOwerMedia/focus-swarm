Everything your kitchen knows, in one list

Built for flatshares, couples and families who are tired of the "do we have any…?" text.

Scan to add

Point your camera at a barcode or a receipt and Larder adds the items with sensible expiry dates. Edit anything with a tap.

Expiry nudges

A gentle push notification the day before something goes off, with three recipes that use it up.

One list, many people

Everyone in the household sees the same pantry in real time. See who added what, and who finished the milk.

Smart shopping list

Larder builds your next shop from what is running low, grouped by aisle, and syncs it to whoever is in the supermarket.

Waste report

A monthly summary of what you threw away and what it cost. Most households save more than the subscription.

Partner offers

Optional discounts from our retail partners on the things you actually buy, based on your pantry.
