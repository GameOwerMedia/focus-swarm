Privacy, in plain words

We built Larder to know what is in your kitchen. Here is what that means for your data.

  • What we collect: the items in your pantry, scanned receipts (store, date, items, prices), your household members' names and emails, and basic usage analytics.
  • Receipts: we keep the receipt image for 30 days to improve recognition, then keep only the extracted items.
  • Partner offers: we may share anonymised and aggregated shopping insights with retail and brand partners to power offers and market research.
  • Household visibility: everyone in a household can see every item and who added it.
  • Deleting your data: email hello@example.com and we will delete your account within 30 days.
